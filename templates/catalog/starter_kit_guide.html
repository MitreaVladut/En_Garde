Beginner & Parent Advice

Your Child's First Fencing Kit Made Simple

Entering a fencing salle for the first time is thrilling. Here is our straightforward, club-approved guide on what safety gear to buy first, in what order, and how to pick the right sizes.

1
Phase 1: Basic Safety

Required for introductory footwork and partner drills in the first 1–3 months:

  • Fencing Glove: Non-slip palm, padded wrist.
  • 350N Mask: Clean vision mesh and neck bib.
  • Underarm Plastron (800N): Mandatory internal puncture protector.
2
Phase 2: Uniform & Weapon

Added when sparring on the strip begins (Months 3–6):

  • 350N Jacket: CE Level 1 with stretch back panel.
  • 350N Breeches: High waist with protective knee cut.
  • Practice or Electric Weapon: Size 0 or 2 for kids under 12; Size 5 for teens & adults.
3
Phase 3: Electrical Gear

Needed for scoring box matches and tournament travel:

  • Body Cords (x2): Always carry a backup cord in the bag.
  • Conductive Lamé: Inox vest for foil or long-sleeve for sabre.
  • Weapon Carrier Bag: Keeps blades dry and safe during commute.

Understanding Safety Numbers (350N vs 800N)

The "N" stands for Newtons—a measure of puncture resistance force. Here is what every parent and fencer needs to know:

Level Fabric Resistance Who is it for? Required By
CE Level 1 (350N) Resists 350 Newtons of puncture force. Beginners, kids, club training, national cadet leagues. Domestic federations across Europe.
FIE Level 2 (800N) Resists 800 Newtons of puncture force (dyneema/ballistic fiber). Competitive fencers, senior circuits, international events. International Fencing Federation (FIE).
